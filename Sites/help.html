How to use Archie

Three buttons to help you explore.

Back to posts

  • Help

    Opens this guide. Use Back to posts or the Archie logo to return to the feed.

    Please note that Archie is still under 🏗

  • Interact

    Turns post interaction on or off. Switch it on to follow links, play videos, and use the controls inside each post.

    Switching the button off lets you grab and scroll, you can skroll as normal on mobile.

  • Shuffle

    Shuffles the posts, on each shuffle you get a new batch, until the posts pool is exhausted.

    Once the pool is exhausted you get random posts. Reloading the page restarts this process

✧ A little space for what stays with you.
